# G02 contract: group volume, trim, mute and channel roles (`ffw-ld0`)

Scope: the control layer above D02's `ProtectedBoardDsp`: group master volume times per-room trim, the
bound on the final gain, mute with a single owner per reason, `(epoch, revision)` command authority,
reconnect and restart behaviour, and `Stereo`/`Left`/`Right`/`Sub` roles. Code:
`crates/franken-dsp/src/volume.rs` and `volume/tests.rs`. Offline and host-only.

**Numerical tests do not prove physical behavior.** Nothing here touches a device, a speaker or a
button. The ramp timings are D02's synthetic 10 ms, observed on the host. Acoustic behaviour,
perceived loudness and real button hardware are separate evidence this module can reference but
never replace. Verification status lives on the bead, not in this file.

## What G02 owns, and what it does not

| Owned by G02 | Owned elsewhere |
|---|---|
| the target gain and the mute decision | the smooth ramp, its exact snap, the protection limiter and fault latch: **D02** |
| checking a command's `(epoch, revision)` against the epoch it was *told* about | choosing epochs, leases, takeover, membership: **G01** (`franken-session`, not touched here) |
| role remap of the input | per-board crossover, EQ, gain ceilings: the board's D02 calibration |

G02 takes the group, epoch and revision as plain inputs. It never adopts an epoch from a command.

## Gain composition, bounded before the ramp

```
requested = (volume / 100) * (trim / 1000)            volume 0..=100, trim 0..=2000 (2.0x)
ceiling   = min(protection ceiling, fault ceiling)    each 0..=1; non-finite becomes 0
target    = min(requested, ceiling), clamped to 0..=1
```

`requested` can exceed 1 (a 2.0x trim); `target` cannot exceed `ceiling`. The member hands D02
`set_user_gain(target)` and `set_muted(any mute reason)`. Because D02 slews its applied gain toward
whatever target it holds, **the ramp is never given a target above the ceilings**, so the applied gain
never exceeds them either. The volume scale is linear in gain: 1 % is -40 dB. A loudness taper is not
specified by this bead and is not implemented.

A group volume change never touches a room's trim; `ResetTrim` does. Local buttons step the trim by
at most 500 per press, clamp to 0..=2000, and never unmute.

## One ramp per change

D02's ramp retargets from its current applied gain and snaps exactly onto the target, so a new target
mid-ramp continues from where the output is: up, further up, or down, without restarting from zero
and without reversing. The vectors and the judge check this on the real renderer (`G02-MBR-002`,
`G02-INV-ATOMIC-RAMP`), including that no control operation steps the output: the applied gain
changes only while the renderer runs.

## Mute: four reasons, one owner each

| Reason (priority order) | Set by | Cleared only by |
|---|---|---|
| `fault` | the board not being `Active` (`observe_board`) | the board being `Active` again |
| `local` | the room's own mute control (`local_mute`) | `local_mute(false)` |
| `join` | a new, restored or rejoining member | `complete_join` |
| `group` | `SetGroupMute(true)` | `SetGroupMute(false)` |

The reported reason is the first that holds. Mute always wins: a volume command is stored but never
unmutes, a group unmute cannot clear `local`, `join` or `fault`, and a mute applies even while a
volume ramp is in flight (the target becomes 0 and the applied gain falls monotonically). If a
requested unmute is overridden, `CommandReceipt.effective_mute` and `MemberView.effective_mute` say
which reason, so a UI can explain silence it was asked not to produce.

## Authority: `(epoch, revision)`

A member holds the epoch it was told (`declare_epoch`, from the membership layer). Checks run in this
order and a refusal changes nothing and consumes no revision:

| Order | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | no epoch declared | `no_authority` |
| 2 | command epoch older | `stale_epoch` |
| 3 | command epoch newer than declared | `undeclared_epoch` (never adopted) |
| 4 | same revision as the last accepted command, same payload | accepted as a **duplicate**, not applied again |
| 5 | same revision, different payload | `revision_conflict` |
| 6 | revision not newer than the last | `stale_revision` |
| 7 | payload out of range | `volume_out_of_range`, `step_out_of_range`, `trim_out_of_range` |

`declare_epoch` of an older epoch is refused; the same epoch keeps revision tracking; a newer epoch
resets it. Steps and values are range-checked, never negated or wrapped (`i16::MIN`, `u8` 255 and
`u16` 65535 are refused, not overflowed).

**What "retries are idempotent" covers, and what it does not.** A replay of the *last* accepted
command is a no-op. A replay of an *older* command, after a newer one was applied, is
`stale_revision`: the member cannot tell it was a retry, and it is refused, which is also safe. There
is no retry scheduler, timeout or acknowledgement in this module.

## Reconnect and restart

* **Rejoin** (`reconnect(snapshot)`): the member takes the group's *current* volume and group mute
  from the snapshot and becomes join-muted. The room's own mute and trim are kept. An older epoch or
  an out-of-range volume is refused with nothing changed.
* **Restart** (`restore`): persisted intent (volume, trim, local mute) comes back, the live session
  does not. The member has no authority and is join-muted, and its bounded gain is **0** until it
  rejoins, so a stored loud volume is never armed, even by a caller that forgot to mute.
* After either, the caller runs `complete_join` once the member is synchronized, then `sync`; the
  first ramp starts from silence and heads for the group's volume only.

## Roles

Roles are an input remap in front of the board; the crossover and EQ stay in D02's calibration.

| Role | Board receives | Available when |
|---|---|---|
| `Stereo` | `(left, right)` | the layout has no subwoofer path |
| `Left` | `(left, left)` | same |
| `Right` | `(right, right)` | same |
| `Sub` | `(m, m)` with `m = 0.5 * (left + right)` | every driver channel is a subwoofer path |

A role the board lacks is refused by name, never silently degraded: `no_audio_output` (zero channels
or no routing, such as the Bridge), `invalid_layout` (routing length differs from the channel count),
`no_subwoofer_path` (`Sub` on a board without one), `subwoofer_only` (`Stereo`/`Left`/`Right` on a pure
sub), `mixed_sub_layout` (a subwoofer among other drivers, which an input remap cannot serve).

## Groups and missing members

`GroupVolume` is the authority's view. A group-wide command is validated on an authority replica
first; a refusal there changes nothing anywhere. Accepted commands, and accepted replays, fan out to
the members that are present; each member then validates independently, so one that was told about a
newer epoch refuses the old command while the others accept it (the authority's state still advances).
Absent members are skipped and rejoin from a fresh snapshot. `SetTrim` and `ResetTrim` are per-room
and refused at the group level (`not_group_wide`). Unknown members (`unknown_member`) and duplicate
members (`duplicate_member`) are refused by name. The fan-out is in process: no network, no ordering
between members.

## Button and UI adapter surface

`MemberVolume::view(&dsp)` returns a `MemberView` that keeps apart: the **desired** volume (persisted
intent), the **requested** gain, the **bounded** target after the ceilings, the **ceiling** itself,
D02's **applied** gain (the only instantaneous value), the effective mute reason, and whether a
ceiling is limiting. `persisted()` stores only the desired volume, trim and local mute, never the
applied gain. No hardware button driver exists in this slice; `local_mute` and `local_trim_step` are
the call surface an adapter would use.

## Using it with D02

After any change (a command, a local control, a reconnect, a join, a board status change) the caller
runs `member.sync(&mut dsp)`: it observes the board status, then sets the bounded gain and the mute.
`set_muted(false)` is only requested when no reason holds, which implies the board is `Active`, so
D02's `unmute_refused` cannot occur from `sync`. Types are not internally synchronized: the caller
serializes access.

## Running, and the evidence boundary

```
RCH_REQUIRE_REMOTE=1 rch exec -- cargo test -p franken-dsp -- --nocapture > dsp.log
scripts/e2e/g02 --tier full --judge dsp.log [--receipts DIR]
```

A full pass plans 74 cases: 4 meta, 45 vectors and 25 Rust tests. The judge reuses the D01 cargo-log
parser. For every vector it (1) compares the Rust observation with the reference model's expectation
(f32 emulated operation by operation, so values agree to the last bit; the tolerance is 1e-7, one ulp
near 1.0) and (2) checks properties **directly on the observation** with arithmetic of its own: the
f64 closed form of the gain, "muted means target 0", "a refusal changes nothing", "a replay is not
applied again", "a command never changes the epoch", "a control operation never steps the output", "the
ramp never moves more than one step", "an absent member is never sent a command". A case or required
Rust test with no observation is `blocked` (exit 3), never passed.

The Rust harness compares each observation with the vector's `expect` itself, so a divergence fails
`cargo test`, not only the judge, and a control test confirms a planted wrong expectation is
detected. A further test fails loudly if D02's synthetic ramp is no longer 10 ms, which would make
every ramp vector stale.

**Planted defects.** `tests/g02/test_g02.py` plants 18 defects in the reference model (for example a
volume command that unmutes, a group unmute that clears a local mute, an epoch adopted from a command,
a retry applied twice, a reconnect that keeps the stored volume, a restored member armed with its stale
volume, a ramp that restarts from zero). For each: the unmutated baseline is green first, the defect
is caught by at least one vector, it is **also** caught by the properties alone with the model's
expectation neutralized, and removing it restores green.

## Limits, stated plainly

* **The model is a second implementation of this contract by the same author.** It catches
  transcription mistakes and regressions; it cannot catch a misreading of the contract that both
  share. Hand-derived pins in `test_g02.py` (numbers worked out on paper) and the model-independent
  properties are the checks on it. An independent reviewer reading this contract against the code is
  worth more than another test.
* The unit tests and vectors have been checked here only by `rustfmt` and `py_compile`; compilation,
  clippy and every Rust result come from the central run, and the vectors' first real Rust execution
  is that run.
* Host-only. No device, speaker, button or network is involved; ramp timings are D02's synthetic
  calibration.
* Linear volume scale (no loudness taper); trim range and step sizes are policy choices of this
  slice.
* Not wired to `franken-session`: epochs and snapshots are plain inputs, and G01's real membership
  layer has not been integrated.
* Role remap is an input remap only: it does not verify that a `Sub` board's calibration contains the
  low-pass the mono mix needs, and it refuses, rather than serves, boards that mix a subwoofer with
  other drivers.
* No retry scheduler, acknowledgement or timeout; no ordering between group members.
