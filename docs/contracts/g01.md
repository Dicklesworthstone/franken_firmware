# Contract G01 — Unified Group Membership State Machine (`ffw-kh0`)

**Status: first slice implemented offline in `crates/franken-session/src/membership.rs`; no central
result yet; the bead is open and blocked in the tracker.** This slice is the section 12 core: the
membership cutover protocol, the authority check on every mutation, the missing-acknowledgement
policy, exclusion as an unresolved output grant, epoch advance, and stable-intent persistence. The
rest of the bead (section 8) is not done. Nothing here touches a device, a network, or a provider,
and no claim is made about which boards or firmware support any of it.

The module is additive. `UnifiedGroupSession::apply_command`, `SessionError` and
`GroupCommandPayload` are unchanged; the engine has its own `MembershipError`. The N02 control
channel and this engine therefore never share a sequence space.

## 1. Authority

A group has one **configured issuer** per incarnation: the session's `coordinator_id`. Every mutation
presents an `AuthorityToken { group_id, issuer_id, epoch, expires_at_ns }`. `AuthorityToken::check`
is pure and refuses, in this order:

| Condition | Error |
|---|---|
| `group_id` differs | `WRONG_GROUP` |
| `issuer_id` is not the configured issuer | `NOT_CONFIGURED_ISSUER` |
| `epoch` below the group's | `STALE_AUTHORITY` |
| `epoch` above the group's | `FUTURE_AUTHORITY` (a follower never mints a valid epoch) |
| `now_ns >= expires_at_ns` | `AUTHORITY_EXPIRED` |

A refused call changes nothing. Token *authenticity* is the transport's job (N01/N02); this layer
decides whether the token is *current*. `advance_epoch` is the only way the group moves to a newer
epoch: it needs the issuer's current token, aborts any proposal in flight (`EPOCH_ADVANCED`), resets
the session's command record (the record belongs to the old epoch) and bumps `state_revision` once.

## 2. The cutover protocol

One change (join or leave) is in flight at a time (`PROPOSAL_PENDING`).

1. **Propose** `(change, cutover_frame, ack_deadline_ns)`. Checked before anything is recorded: the
   member is absent (join) or present (leave), a joiner's board is eligible, the group is not full
   (`MAX_GROUP_MEMBERS` = 32, provisional), a joiner has no unresolved earlier output, the cutover is
   at least `min_cutover_lead_frames` (4,800, provisional) ahead, and the deadline is in the future.
   **Nothing audible changes.**
2. **Ack** from each participant (every current member, plus the joiner): `ReadinessReport` with
   `format_ok`, `buffer_ok`, `clock_confident`, `can_stop_flush` and the frame at which the member's
   irrevocable already-submitted output ends. A repeat of the same report is idempotent; a different
   one is `ACK_CONFLICT`; a stranger is `NOT_A_PARTICIPANT`; no new ack is accepted after the deadline.
3. **Commit** needs every non-excluded participant acknowledged (`MISSING_ACKS`) and ready
   (`MEMBER_NOT_READY`), the cutover still ahead (`CUTOVER_PASSED`), and the cutover not before any
   member's output tail (`CUTOVER_BEFORE_OUTPUT_TAIL`). **Still nothing audible changes.**
4. **Activate** at the first `tick` with `frame >= cutover_frame`: the change is applied to the
   session in one step and `state_revision` increases by exactly 1. A joiner enters
   `SynchronizedReady` (it validated clock confidence in its report). If the group changed so that the
   committed change no longer applies, it aborts with `PRECONDITION_FAILED`.

Members, their lifecycle states and `state_revision` are byte-identical through steps 1 to 3. A
proposal can be aborted by the authority at any time before activation (`BY_AUTHORITY`), which leaves
the session exactly as it was: a cancelled join never disturbs playing members. An uncommitted
proposal whose cutover frame arrives lapses (`CUTOVER_MISSED`), so an authority that never commits
cannot hold the group. Settled results stay queryable by proposal id for the last 64 proposals
(`MAX_TERMINAL_HISTORY`); older ids answer `UNKNOWN_PROPOSAL`, explicitly.

## 3. Missing acknowledgements, exclusion, and unresolved output

At the ack deadline `tick` applies the configured `MissingAckPolicy`:

- `Abort` (default): the proposal aborts, the group is unchanged.
- `ExcludeUnreachable`: existing members that did not acknowledge are excluded; the proposal
  continues without them and removes them at the cutover.
- An unreachable **joiner** aborts under either policy.

An excluded member is **not** evidence of silence. Each one is recorded as an unresolved output grant
with an unknown bound. `conflicting_start_gate(now_frame)` refuses a conflicting program while any
such grant is unknown or its bound lies ahead; `resolve_output` records a stop acknowledgement or a
conservative expiry bound (the caller supplies output expiry plus irrevocable tail, clock uncertainty
and stop/drain lead). Evidence can never shorten a bound already required, and an excluded member
cannot be re-admitted while its output is unresolved (`OUTPUT_UNRESOLVED`).

## 4. Persistence and restart

Only `MembershipIntent { group_id, issuer_id, members: [{ member_id, profile_id, role }] }` is
persisted. It has no lease, queue, acknowledgement, lifecycle or volume. After a restart,
`GroupMembership::after_restart` refuses every mutation (`AUTHORITY_NOT_DISCOVERED`) until
`discover_authority` is given a token the live group accepts. A restored intent lists members to
re-admit (`members_missing_from`); it is not a restored live session. How and where the intent is
stored is U01's decision.

## 5. Interaction with command traffic

Membership activation and epoch advance bump `state_revision` like an applied command, so a client
holding an older expected revision is refused by the N02 channel's revision check. That is intended.
Neither consumes a `command_seq`.

## 6. Verification

| Invariant | Case (all in `membership.rs`, hand-written expectations unless noted) |
|---|---|
| a proposal never alters audible output; activation exactly once, at the frame | `a_join_changes_nothing_audible_until_its_cutover_frame_and_then_exactly_once`, `a_leave_removes_the_member_only_at_its_cutover_frame` |
| stale / future / foreign / expired authority is refused and changes nothing | `every_mutation_refuses_a_token_that_is_not_current_and_changes_nothing` |
| planted: a commit under a stale epoch | `a_commit_under_a_stale_epoch_is_refused_and_the_epoch_advance_aborted_the_proposal` |
| planted: a missing ack | `a_missing_ack_blocks_the_commit_then_the_deadline_aborts_without_holding_the_group`, `a_missing_joiner_always_aborts_...` |
| exclusion leaves output unresolved; unknown bound refuses | `excluding_an_unreachable_member_continues_the_cutover_but_leaves_its_output_unresolved` |
| planted: a duplicate commit; conflicting acks; strangers | `duplicate_commits_conflicting_acks_and_strangers_are_refused` |
| the cutover cannot precede an output tail; cannot be late | `the_cutover_may_not_precede_...`, `a_commit_at_or_after_the_cutover_frame_is_refused_...` |
| cancellation leaves playback intact; not-ready members block | `cancelling_a_committed_join_...`, `a_member_that_is_not_ready_...` |
| one change at a time; preconditions before anything is recorded | `one_change_at_a_time_and_every_precondition_is_checked_...` |
| only stable intent persisted; restart rediscovers authority | `only_stable_intent_is_persisted_and_a_restart_must_rediscover_authority` |
| bounded history | `terminal_results_are_kept_for_reconciliation_but_the_history_is_bounded` |
| independence from the command record | `membership_activation_is_independent_of_the_command_record_...` |
| all of the above under reorder, retry, restart and epoch schedules | `seeded_schedules_keep_every_membership_invariant` (8 seeded schedules of 600 steps; checks after every step that a refused call changed nothing, that a pending proposal never altered members or revision, that activation happens only at or after its frame and once, that settled results never change, and, against an independent model, that the unresolved-output set and the conflicting-start gate are exactly what the exclusions and recorded evidence imply, that an excluded member can neither acknowledge nor rejoin, and that it is no longer in the group). Left to chance the generator almost never reached exclusion (the cutover frame and the random abort end nearly every proposal before its ack deadline), so the exclusion-policy seeds open with a scripted exclusion and later scripts re-run it with a random unreachable member; the floors are activations >= 5, aborts >= 5, and at least one activated exclusion per exclusion-policy seed (4 seeds) |

Command: `RCH_REQUIRE_REMOTE=1 rch exec -- cargo test -p franken-session` (expect 9 + 17 = 26 passed),
then `cargo clippy -p franken-session --all-targets -- -D warnings`. These are host unit tests on a
virtual timeline; they say nothing about hardware, real clocks, or networks.

## 7. Provisional constants

`MAX_GROUP_MEMBERS` 32, `MAX_TERMINAL_HISTORY` 64 and `min_cutover_lead_frames` 4,800 (100 ms at
48 kHz) are engineering placeholders, not measured or owner-approved figures.

## 8. Not done (stays open)

- **Source ownership arbitration** across Spotify, AirPlay and line-in, with an explicit
  replace/reject policy per command class; replaced sources cannot publish; concurrent
  starts/seeks/group changes admitting one source epoch.
- **The full prior-grant-holder oracle.** Only grants created by exclusion are tracked; grants issued
  by source changes, disconnected members outside a cutover, and output authorization deadlines
  enforced by receivers (including queued samples) are not modelled.
- **Partition and failover.** One configured issuer only; no lease renewal protocol, no quorum, no
  two-follower partition model, no issuer-restart expiry guard.
- **Integration.** Frames and nanoseconds are supplied by the caller; wiring to N03's clock transform,
  D03 sample positions, the HAL's irrevocable-tail report, the speaker's virtual house, and the N02
  channel (a later lane) is not done. The existing `AdmitMember`/`RemoveMember` commands still work
  directly and are not yet gated behind this protocol.
- **Evidence pipeline.** `tests/g01/` named cases, an independent reference with golden scenarios,
  `scripts/e2e/g01`, the competing-adapters script, and Q00/Q07 registration.
- **Persistence** itself (U01) and any hardware or provider qualification.
